*Written August 21, 2026*

Since I started some of studies about Agent Engineering, I realized something what I work for, and what is main abilities in my job, developer. When I got a job for developer, I think type a 'easy-readable' code is required to this job. But, this is ridiculous after all! Since Developer do the job between person and computer, Their main job will include communication. And They have to resolve the problem what their employer want to make, so problem structuring and resolving problem must be in it. Size of Company which they work for would be variant for this. There're is few small sized Company have a job which work for Company-wide service division like DBA, Server Engineer, Infra Engineer. So their developer have to be expert to this knowledge, at least in their Company-wide service. These make developer be a student about everything. They should study every day, and ask in community aka stacktrace, and discuss with other developer for resolving their problem what other developer may resolved before. I think, this things make exhausted most of developers who do not adapt to job yet.

But now, they can have a strong tool to take this abilities, LLM Agent. They can discuss when ever you want, search for new tech stack, make you understand this easily and analyze logs what're too long to read. This makes you can catch up tech stack and use it for your job like dev-ops, automated skills. Of course, their would be some issue like fear of job substitution by AI, FOMO and lazy-company-culture for shifting work-flow to ai-friendly work-flow. but I think, After all, some how, It will be our job environment.

So we could ready for it. AI could make's our work which developer do with computer faster than ever, dramaticaly. Then our main work would be connect person to computer with AI. As I said, in this moment we should take a communicate with companions, make a easily understandable problem structure with problem which get from meeting with companion and express it logically and specifically to make understand your companion and AI.

So developer's have to talk, and think about problem more than AI do. They have to do the role names 'final-harness' which make's AI stop a work what they not allowed. 

Last of all, Here's the thing. Be a chatterbox, developer.
