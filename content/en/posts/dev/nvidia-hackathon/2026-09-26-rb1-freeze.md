The last gate before running the sealed sets was freezing the rulebook. After the freeze, the scoring rules can only change with a new version and a stated reason.

---

## Background: g1 Was Only a Skeleton

At design time, the plan was to pick comparison countries with the similarity grouping `g1`. But `g1` was only a skeleton. Putting it in right before the freeze would change investigation behavior on real data, and there was no time to validate that. The MVP had been running from the start on the fixed comparison-country list `g0` (the top 5 countries by 2023 imports among the 15 countries other than the target country).

## Three Decisions

My answer was: "Replace with g0, put in prose rule EX-2 as an extension. And for the prohibited expression, I'll go with the recommendation and not add it."

| Item | Chosen | Rejected | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| Comparison countries | **Declare `g0` as the replacement** | Freeze `g1` | No time to validate `g1` |
| Prose rule EX-2 | **Extend** (the scorer also excludes the electrical-unit and item-code expressions the validator already excludes) | As is (the orchestrator's recommendation) | There were 0 matching cases in actual reports and traces, so this isn't tweaking things to favor the results |
| Prohibited expression "via a third country" | **Not added** | Add | Likewise, 0 matching cases |

For EX-2, I chose differently from the orchestrator's recommendation. I thought aligning the validator and the scorer was better than freezing with the two rules out of step. It was a choice made possible because there was evidence (0 matching cases) that it wouldn't affect the results.

## The Freeze

Next, the appendix's 13 user-confirmation items came up. Each item had a different option.

- Make the claim-level error rate, instead of the report-level one, the primary metric
- Make a paired test, instead of non-overlapping intervals, the primary determination
- No reruns for infrastructure failures

The recommendation for all of them was to keep the current rules. My answer was: "Keep all. Freeze confirmed." The three freeze conditions were met earlier than the scheduled time (18:00), so we moved it up. We froze the rulebook body, the scorer, the prose pattern list, the judgment policy, the validator, the digit table, and the run-order seed together.

## What This Choice Meant

Declaring `g0` as the replacement is **writing down publicly that we didn't build everything we planned**. In the results table and the submission, we stated that "`g1` was planned but not implemented, and `g0` was used." Not writing about what we couldn't build as if we had built it: that was another name for the freeze.
